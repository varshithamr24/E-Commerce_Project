11. Business Insights & Recommendations

🏆 Top-Selling Product — Laptop
📌 Finding: Laptop drives ₹7,15,315 (51.9% of total revenue). Mouse is the weakest product at ₹12,322 (0.9%).
💡 Recommendation: Invest in Laptop inventory and bundled promotions (e.g. Laptop + Keyboard + Mouse). Reassess Mouse's pricing and visibility strategy.
📦 Category Revenue Concentration Risk
📌 Finding: Electronics contributes 89.3% of revenue — Wearables (5.4%) and Accessories (5.3%) are severely underweighted.
💡 Recommendation: Expand the product range within Wearables and Accessories. Introduce new SKUs and dedicated category promotions to reduce concentration risk.
🗺️ Regional Performance Gap
📌 Finding: West leads with ₹4,05,964 but has the highest return rate (39.5%). South has the lowest revenue (₹3,03,723) and lowest return rate (26.2%).
💡 Recommendation: Urgently investigate West's 39.5% return rate. Replicate North's high average order value (₹1,470) strategy in South to close the ₹1,02,241 revenue gap.
↩️ Return Rate Alert — 30.8%
📌 Finding: 308 orders returned representing ₹4,25,823 in reversed revenue. Headphones has the highest return rate (33.3%), followed by Mouse (32.4%).
💡 Recommendation: Improve product descriptions, photography, and sizing guides. Introduce real-time tracking and post-purchase follow-up to reduce returns. Every 1% reduction = ₹13,788 saved.
👥 Customer Loyalty Opportunity
📌 Finding: 86% repeat rate is strong, but the total base of 292 customers is very small. Average spend per customer is ₹4,722.
💡 Recommendation: Launch a tiered loyalty programme (points, early access, personalised discounts). Simultaneously invest in digital marketing and referral programmes to grow the customer base.
🎂 High-Value Age Segment — 46–55
📌 Finding: The 46–55 age group generates the highest revenue (₹4,54,231, 32.9%) with 286 orders. The 18–25 segment is the smallest at ₹1,19,044.
💡 Recommendation: Tailor email campaigns, push notifications, and product curation for 46–55. Develop entry-level products and value bundles to attract the 18–25 segment.
📅 Seasonal Sales Peaks
📌 Finding: January (₹1,53,523, 114 orders) is the peak month. Q4 is the strongest quarter (₹3,83,405). February and March are the weakest months.
💡 Recommendation: Pre-stock inventory before January and Q4. Run clearance deals and discounts in February/March to smooth the seasonal revenue trough.
📈 Average Order Value Uplift
📌 Finding: Current average order value is ₹1,379. North has the highest regional AOV at ₹1,470; South has the lowest at ₹1,245.
💡 Recommendation: Implement "Frequently Bought Together" bundles, a free-shipping threshold (e.g. ₹2,000), and volume-discount tiers (buy 3+ save 10%) to raise AOV across all regions.
🚚 Shipping & Delivery Improvement
📌 Finding: Only 31.3% of orders are confirmed delivered. 32.9% remain in transit and 30.8% are returned — meaning less than a third of orders complete successfully.
💡 Recommendation: Partner with faster logistics providers. Add real-time tracking, proactive delay notifications, and clear estimated delivery dates at checkout to improve the delivery completion rate.
